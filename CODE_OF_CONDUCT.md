# Community code of conduct

SOVRAN welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. In project issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other project spaces:

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Make room for different experience levels, perspectives, and communication styles.
- Respect privacy. Do not share another person's personal information or confidential code, credentials, or logs.
- Do not engage in harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized behavior, personal attacks, or persistent disruption.
- Accept maintainer decisions and reasonable requests to change behavior.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers apply these expectations consistently. They may edit or remove inappropriate content, close or lock
discussions, limit participation, or remove a contributor from project spaces. The response should reflect the
severity and persistence of the behavior. Mistakes made in good faith should normally begin with guidance.

## Reporting

For conduct problems, use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report user** controls when appropriate, and block unwanted
contact. For routine moderation, you may ask a maintainer to review a thread without repeating harmful or private
details. The project does not currently publish a separate confidential conduct-reporting address. Do not post
personal information publicly. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
